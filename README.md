# Codex Companion

Ask a Destiny 2 question, get an answer a new or returning player can act on, with
the data it came from attached.

Weapons, damage math, exotic armor, aspects and fragments, raids and dungeons come
from Bungie's manifest and community spreadsheets. Patch notes and the wiki are
retrieved by meaning, not keywords. Every answer names its sources and states how
old the data is.

> Status: in development. Design and plan are in `docs/superpowers/`.

## What it is not

Not a replacement for the tools that already do their job well. It points at them
instead: DIM for loadouts, d2armorpicker for armor stats, destiny.report and light.gg
for god rolls, raid.report and dungeon.report for clears, braytech for progress.

## Data credits

Community data stays with its authors. This repository credits and links to it, and
never redistributes the source sheets.
